# Pleco Lab

Create a world-class modern SaaS website UI for **Pleco Lab**, a premium technology solutions company helping SMEs across **India, Africa, and Asia** scale through technology.

**Brand positioning:**

Pleco Lab builds custom websites, CRM systems, business automation, AI solutions, WhatsApp integrations, internal tools, and custom software. We also have deep expertise in **travel, immigration, education, and service businesses**, but the brand should feel broad enough for all industries.

**Design inspiration:**

Take strong inspiration from **Dropship.io**, **Vercel**, **Linear**, and premium modern SaaS startups.

**Design direction:**

* Full dark premium theme (NO white sections)

* Clean enterprise SaaS feel

* Ultra modern

* Minimal but high-converting

* Conversion-focused layout

* Premium typography

* Smooth gradients

* Glassmorphism where appropriate

* Subtle animations

* High trust B2B SaaS design

* Deep navy / black background

* Electric blue + violet accent glows

* Professional, not flashy cyberpunk

**Color palette:**

Background: #060B1A / midnight navy

Accent: electric blue / purple gradients

Text: white / soft gray

Cards: dark glassmorphism with subtle borders

**Website objective:**

This website should make SMEs, immigration firms, travel companies, education consultancies, healthcare businesses, and service businesses feel that Pleco Lab is a premium technology partner.
